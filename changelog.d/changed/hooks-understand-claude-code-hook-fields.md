- **Hooks understand Claude Code's hook fields.** Besides its own keys, a hook's input now carries `hook_event_name`, `tool_name`, `tool_input`, `tool_use_id` and, after a call, `tool_response`, and its answer may use `hookSpecificOutput` with `permissionDecision` (`allow`, `deny`, `ask`, `defer`), `permissionDecisionReason`, `updatedInput` or `updatedToolOutput`, the legacy `"approve"`, and after a call `decision: "block"` with a `reason`. Existing hooks work as before.
