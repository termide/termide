- **Menus open with no item chosen.** A menu bar dropdown, a project button's menu, a panel's `[≡]` menu (`Alt+K`), an operation's menu and the stash menu open with nothing selected, so `←`/`→` walk the menu bar without stepping into a submenu such as `AI → Agents` or `Options → Themes` on the way. `↓` chooses the first item and `↑` the last; `Enter` does nothing until an item is chosen, so `Alt+K Enter` and `F9 Enter` now take a `↓` first. A submenu opened from an item with `▶` still starts on its first item.
