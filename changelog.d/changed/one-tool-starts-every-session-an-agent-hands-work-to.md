- **One tool starts every session an agent hands work to.** `task` and `fork` are replaced by `start_session`, which says on three axes what the new session is: what it knows (`context`: `none`, the prompt alone, or `fork`, a copy of this conversation), whether its answer comes back (`reply`: `wait`, `later` or `none`) and where you see it (`show`: `background` or `panel`). `agents` is renamed `sessions`. Combinations that do not hold together — a call waiting on a panel, a fork in a panel that reports back, `focus` on a fresh session — are refused with what to change. A message to a session and a delegate's answer now go through one path, to the panel the session is open in or else its log. The old names are unknown tools now: rename `task` and `fork` to `start_session`, and `agents` to `sessions`, in an agent's `tools` list and in permission rules (`[ai.permissions.task]` rules become `[ai.permissions.start_session]` ones, a fork's rule reading `"fork: *"`). Their texts in `ai/tools/` are deleted on the next start unless you edited them, as is `bash.md`, left from before `bash` became `shell`. Session logs that used the old tools still show and carry on as before. See [Starting sessions](doc/en/agent.md#starting-sessions).
