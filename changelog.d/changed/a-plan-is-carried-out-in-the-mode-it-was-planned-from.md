- **A plan is carried out in the mode you worked in before planning.** The plan card's first two rows went to `edit`, which sets the configured `allow` rules aside, so every command of the plan asked, even one allowed for good. They now go back to the mode you last sent a message in outside plan mode (`auto`, say), named on the row; the modes `Shift+Tab` passes through on the way to plan do not count. From `ask`, or in a session that started in plan mode, they go to `configured`. Accepting edits stays on offer as the third row, and the separate "configured rules" row is gone.
