- **`find` with a dotfile pattern no longer asks as a read outside the project.** The agent took the values of `find`'s expression for paths, so `find . -not -path '*/.git*'` or `find . -name '.env*'` asked, even in plan mode, as if they could read `..`. Only the starting points and the files `-newer`, `-anewer`, `-cnewer`, `-samefile` and `-files0-from` name are now judged as paths.
