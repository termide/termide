- **A click on a dialog's button lands where the button is drawn.** The confirmation, choice, Save As, bookmark and rename dialogs placed their buttons' click areas by the bytes of the labels, not the columns they take, and centred them a column off ratatui's drawing: in most languages but English a click could miss a button or hit its neighbour, Delete's confirmation included. The areas now follow the drawing, and a confirmation is as wide as its buttons in the language.
