- **Regex search and replace keep line breaks.** Each line was searched with its line break attached, so Replace all of `\s+$` with nothing joined the whole file into one line and `foo$` matched only on the last line. Lines are now matched without their line break. Replace after a replacement that adds or removes lines goes to the next real match instead of a stale position, Replace all is one undo step, and a regex match is highlighted over its own length rather than the pattern's.
