- **A plugin's status bar items name their plugin, and stay within bounds.** Each item shows as `<plugin>: <text>`, the Perforce one as `perforce: #3/5 (out of date)` where it read `p4: …`. Its text, like a command's title, is cleaned of control and invisible characters and cut to length (80 characters for an item, 60 for a title), and a plugin holds at most 8 items, so one plugin can no longer push the rest of the status bar off the screen or forge another's item.
