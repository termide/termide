- **Saving settings keeps a linked `config.toml` linked.** A save wrote a fixed `config.toml.tmp` and renamed it over the file, which replaced a symlink — a config kept in a dotfiles checkout — with a plain copy, left the data unsynced before the rename, and let two saves at once share one temporary file. Settings now go through the atomic write the editor and the agent use: a uniquely named, synced sibling renamed over the link's target.
