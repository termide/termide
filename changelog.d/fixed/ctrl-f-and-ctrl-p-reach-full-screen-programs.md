- **`Ctrl+F` and `Ctrl+P` reach full-screen programs in a terminal.** The terminal took `Ctrl+F` for its find bar and the command palette took `Ctrl+P` even while `less`, vim, fzf or emacs had the screen, where they page down, complete or move. With the alternate screen up both keys now go to the program, while at a shell prompt they still open the find bar and the palette. The terminal guide now lists the readline `Alt` keys that global actions take.
