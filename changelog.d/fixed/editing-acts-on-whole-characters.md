- **Editing acts on whole characters.** Backspace and Delete removed one code point of a flag, an emoji with a skin tone or a letter with a combining accent, leaving half of it behind, and undo then put it back in the wrong place; they now remove the whole character. Copy and Cut after such a character put different text on the clipboard than Cut deleted, and the same text went to the agent; they now take exactly the selected text. Double- and triple-click selection, the bracket-pair Backspace, smart indent between brackets and the completion prefix were off by the same amount after such characters and are fixed too, and pasting text that ends in a line break leaves the cursor at the start of the next line.
