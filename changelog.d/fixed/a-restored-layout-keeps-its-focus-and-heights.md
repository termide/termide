- **A restored layout keeps its focus and panel heights.** Panels that are not saved with the layout — plugin panels, the operations panel, an empty editor — and panels that could not be reopened on the next start shifted the others, so after a restart the wrong panel or column could have the focus, custom panel heights were dropped and a column's fullscreen state could come back wrong. The focus and the heights now follow the panels that are saved and reopened, and a column left with none is dropped without moving the focus.
