- **An ACP agent's searches, deletions and moves follow the `read` and `edit` rules.** A search Codex or Gemini CLI asked about was refused in plan mode, and a deletion or a move slipped past a `deny` rule on editing the files it named, since none of these kinds matched a rule. A search is now judged as a `read` of the folder it searches, and a deletion or a move is refused by whatever would refuse editing one of its files; otherwise it is still asked about.
