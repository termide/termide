- **Status bars, the find bar and messages speak the interface language.** The status bars of the editor, the hex viewer, the Markdown, HTML, diagram, CSV and image viewers, the find and replace bar, the command palette, the go-to-line and rename prompts, the cancelled-copy and bookmark dialogs, and the messages about stashes, staging, commits, deletes, code actions, plugins and failed opens were English in every language; they now follow the interface language. One pasted line or one copied character also reads as singular in German, Spanish, French and Portuguese ("1 Zeile", "1 carácter" rather than "1 Zeilen", "1 caracteres"), and a few labels left in English in Indonesian, German and Russian are translated.
