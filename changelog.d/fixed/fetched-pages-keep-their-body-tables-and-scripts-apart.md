- **A fetched page keeps its body, its tables and its scripts apart.** The agent's `fetch` dropped a whole page whose `<head>` had no end tag, read the inside of `<script>` and `<style>` as markup (so a `"<b>"` in a script leaked into the text), and lost table cells whose `</td>` or `</tr>` was left out, the links inside cells, and everything after a table nested in a cell. Raw-text elements are now read as text and skipped, the head ends where the body starts, and cells close as browsers close them, keeping their links, with an inner table flattened into its cell.
