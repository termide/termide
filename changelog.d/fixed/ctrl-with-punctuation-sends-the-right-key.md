- **Ctrl with punctuation and digits sends the right key in the terminal.** `Ctrl+/`, `Ctrl+]`, `Ctrl+\`, `Ctrl+Space`, `Ctrl+?` and `Ctrl+2`…`Ctrl+8` sent unrelated control codes (`Ctrl+/` arrived as `Ctrl+O`); they now send what xterm does, so undo in Emacs, `telnet`'s escape and the like work. Insert now reaches the program, and Delete, Page Up/Down and F1–F12 carry Shift, Alt and Ctrl.
