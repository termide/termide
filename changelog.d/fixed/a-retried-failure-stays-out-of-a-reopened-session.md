- **A retried failure stays out of a reopened session.** Trying a failed request again leaves the failed reply out of the model's context, but the session log kept it: a reopened session sent it again, and a later compaction kept one message too many from the end, which could start at a tool result without its call and be rejected by the provider. The log now records the reply as left out, and a reopen replays the same context.
