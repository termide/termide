- **A loop ends on its condition, or when the agent stops it.** `/loop [interval] <prompt> until: <condition>` has the `/goal` judge check the condition after each run: met, the loop ends with the reason; out of reach, it ends with a warning; otherwise the next run goes as scheduled. `suggest_loop` offers a loop with such a condition, and the agent's `jobs` tool lists the panel's loop under `loop` and stops it once it is no longer needed, with a notice saying so — rather than letting it run on to its 100-run cap.
