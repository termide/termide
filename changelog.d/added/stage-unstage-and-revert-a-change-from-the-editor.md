- **Stage, unstage and revert a change from the editor.** `F7` and `Shift+F7` step through the changes the gutter marks; on a changed line, or on selected lines, the panel's `[≡]` menu stages the change as the editor holds it (saved or not), unstages a staged one, or reverts the lines to the index's version as an ordinary edit `Ctrl+Z` takes back. A staged change's line number is drawn hollow — its colour on the digits, no background — so staged and unstaged changes are told apart. The actions are `stage_change`, `unstage_change` and `revert_change` under `[editor.keybindings]`, with no default key. See [Editor](doc/en/editor.md).
