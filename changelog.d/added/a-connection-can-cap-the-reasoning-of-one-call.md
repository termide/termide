- **A connection can cap the reasoning of one model call.** `reasoning_budget` on a connection, also **Reasoning budget, tokens** on its page in the settings window, bounds how many tokens one call may spend reasoning: a model that reasons past it is cut there and asked again at once without reasoning, rather than reasoning on for minutes until the output limit stops it with nothing to show. 0, the default, sets no limit; termide's own loop only.
