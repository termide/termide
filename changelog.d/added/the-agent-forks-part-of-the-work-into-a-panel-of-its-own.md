- **The agent forks part of the work into a panel of its own.** The new `fork` tool copies the agent's session into a new agent panel, where the copy takes the part of the work the agent wrote for it as its first request. With a `focus`, the copy is first compacted on it, so it starts with what bears on its part rather than the whole history. The copy is named after its own part — the `title` the agent gives it, or the first line of its request — and that name is its panel's title, so several forks of one session are told apart. The fork is yours from the start, unlike a subagent: a session like any other, on the panel's usual rules, with no report back and no limit on its model calls, staying until you close its panel. It asks in `ask` mode, is refused in plan mode, and its first request counts among the messages agents pass one another without you. See [Forking part of the work](doc/en/agent.md#forking-part-of-the-work).
