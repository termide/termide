- **The limit of messages agents pass one another without you is a setting, and asks before it refuses.** `max_agent_messages` under `[ai]`, also on the settings window's AI page under Permissions, sets how many — 10 by default as before, `0` for no limit; open panels take a change at once. Past it, the agent asks you on a card whether to allow as many more, which starts the count again as a message of yours would; stopping or dismissing the card refuses the message as before. See [Agent](doc/en/agent.md).
