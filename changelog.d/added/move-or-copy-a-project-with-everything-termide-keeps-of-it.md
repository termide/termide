- **Move or copy a project with everything Termide keeps of it.** `F6` on a project in the `Projects` menu or in its menu-bar button's menu moves the project's directory, and `F5` copies it. Its saved layout, unsaved buffers, agent sessions with their checkpoints and breakpoints go along, and a move also takes the permissions granted to it. The files go through a background operation. An open project closes for the move and opens again at the new path. A copy opens in the background beside the original, with its own session ids and no permissions granted. On a project marked ✗, whose directory was moved outside Termide, `F6` or `Enter` asks where it is now and carries the same state there, no file moved.
