- **A fork can run out of sight and report back.** A fork no longer has to be a panel of its own that answers no one: `start_session` with `context` `fork` runs a copy of the agent's session out of sight, as a delegate does, on the session's own model, compacted on its `focus` first when given. The agent waits for its answer, or with `reply` `later` goes on and reads it as a message once it comes; `reply` `none` brings nothing back. It is followed, paused, stopped and carried on as any delegate, from its row above the input and with `jobs`. A session on an external agent or a CLI connection still forks into a panel only. See [Starting sessions](doc/en/agent.md#starting-sessions).
