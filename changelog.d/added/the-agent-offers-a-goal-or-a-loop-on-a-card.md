- **The agent offers a goal or a loop on a card.** Instead of writing out a `/goal` or `/loop` line for you to paste, the agent puts it on a card with the new `suggest_goal` and `suggest_loop` tools: the goal, or the loop's prompt and how often it runs, why it is offered, and the goal or loop under way it would replace. **Start** starts it as the typed line would, **Edit first** puts the line in the prompt, **Don't start** or `Esc` declines; nothing starts without the card. Codex and Gemini CLI are served both tools beside `suggest_command`. See [Tools](doc/en/agent.md#tools).
