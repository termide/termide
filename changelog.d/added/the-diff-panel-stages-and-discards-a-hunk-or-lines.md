- **The git diff panel stages, unstages and discards a hunk or lines.** A cursor walks the diff's rows: `Space` stages the file, hunk or line under it, or the lines selected with `V` or `Shift+↑↓`, and unstages them on the staged side; `D` discards them from the working tree after asking, and `U` brings the last discard back while the panel is open; `[` and `]` jump between hunks. Untracked, renamed and binary files go whole. `Space` no longer folds a file there (`Enter` does), except in a commit's or a stash's diff. See [UI](doc/en/ui.md).
