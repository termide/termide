guideline.1: For work of three steps or more, write a checklist with `checklist` before you start and keep it current: a step in progress when you start it, done as soon as it is; skip it for a task of one or two steps.
---
Write the checklist of the work at hand: the whole list each time, every step with its `status` — `pending`, `in_progress` or `done`. Each call replaces the list; add, split, reorder or drop steps as the work shows them, and an empty list clears it. Keep one step in progress at a time. The user sees the list above the input, checked off as you go; while steps remain you are reminded of it at each turn, so it outlives a compaction. It changes nothing in the project and asks no one.
